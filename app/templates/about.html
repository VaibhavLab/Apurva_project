MindCare is an educational Mental Health Chatbot App that connects emotional reflection with relevant wellbeing resources.

{{ icon('chat') }}

Support, at your pace.

Start a conversation, share a thought, and receive a supportive response. Return to past conversations or delete them from your account whenever you choose.

The chatbot uses built-in response patterns, with variations based on the topic and emotional tone. It does not understand you as a person would.

{{ icon('wave') }}

How sentiment analysis works

VADER scores the language in each message from −1 to +1. Scores of 0.05 or above are labeled Positive; −0.05 or below, Negative; and values in between, Neutral.

This is an estimate of the text's tone, not your mental health. Sarcasm, context, and mixed feelings can be misread.

{{ icon('play') }}

A helpful next step

Topic detection looks for themes such as sleep, academic pressure, stress, and motivation. Alongside sentiment, it selects up to three relevant resource searches.

Resources open YouTube search results. Results can change and are not individually reviewed or endorsed. You choose what feels useful.

{{ icon('spark') }}

Built for learning

Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, MySQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and VADER power this project. SQLite is supported for a convenient local demo.

Rasa NLU configuration is included for experimentation. The working demo uses the integrated Flask service layer; a Rasa server is not active.

PRIVACY

Your words deserve care.

Conversations are saved to this application's database and protected by your account. Passwords are hashed. Message analysis runs locally, without sending chat content to an external AI provider.

This academic demo does not encrypt messages at rest. The database operator can access stored data, so avoid sharing identifying or sensitive information. Deleting a conversation removes its messages from the active database; deployment backups, if any, are managed separately. Opening resources sends you to an external service with its own privacy policy.

{{ icon('shield') }}
SUPPORT HAS ITS LIMITS

Your safety comes first.

MindCare is an educational wellness-support application. It does not provide medical diagnosis, professional therapy, or emergency mental-health services.

A separate English phrase check flags some explicit self-harm language and displays urgent support guidance instead of ordinary resources. It can miss risk or flag language incorrectly. Never rely on it to assess safety.

If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a crisis-support service now. Reach out to someone you trust and stay with another person if possible. This chatbot cannot provide emergency assistance.
